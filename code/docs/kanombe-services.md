# Kanombe Health Clinic — Services

## Vaccination

Childhood vaccinations are given every Tuesday morning between 08:00 and 12:00.
Parents should bring the child's vaccination card. Walk-ins are accepted; no
appointment is needed for vaccination.

## Pharmacy

The clinic pharmacy is open during clinic opening hours. It stocks the
essential medicines list. Prescriptions written by outside doctors are
accepted if they are dated within the last thirty days.

## Testing and screening

HIV testing and counselling is available every weekday and is free and
confidential. Blood pressure and blood sugar screening are offered without
appointment on Thursday afternoons.

## Referrals

Serious cases are referred to Kanombe Military Hospital. The clinic arranges
transport for referred emergency patients. Referral letters are issued at
reception and are valid for seven days.

## Counselling

Mental health counselling is available by appointment on Monday and Wednesday
afternoons. Appointments are made at reception or on the general number
0788 555 010.
