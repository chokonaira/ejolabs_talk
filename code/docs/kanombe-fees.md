# Kanombe Health Clinic — Fees

Effective 1 January 2026. Revised annually.

## Consultation

A standard consultation costs 3,000 RWF for a patient without insurance.
With a valid mutuelle de santé card the patient pays 10% of that amount.

## Laboratory tests

A malaria rapid diagnostic test costs 1,500 RWF. A full blood count costs
5,000 RWF. Results for both are available the same day.

## Maternity

Antenatal consultations are free of charge for all patients, with or without
insurance. A normal delivery at the clinic costs 15,000 RWF.

## Payment

Payment is accepted in cash and by mobile money. The clinic does not accept
bank cards. A receipt is issued for every payment.
