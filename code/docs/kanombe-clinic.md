# Kanombe Health Clinic — General Information

## Opening hours

The clinic is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00. On Saturday it is open
08:00 to 13:00. It is closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

## Emergency contact

If you need help at the weekend, at night, or on a public holiday, call the
emergency number: 0788 123 456. The clinic building is closed at those times,
but the emergency number is answered by the duty nurse, 24 hours a day.

## General reception

For appointments, records and billing, call reception on 0788 555 010 during
opening hours. Reception is not staffed outside those hours. Do not use it for
anything urgent.

## Location

The clinic is in Kanombe, Kicukiro District, next to Kanombe Primary School.
The entrance for patients is on the north side of the building. There is a
separate entrance for ambulances on the east side.

## Registration

A new patient must bring a national ID and their community-based health
insurance (mutuelle de santé) card if they have one. Registration takes about
ten minutes and is done at the reception desk before consultation.

## Staff on duty

The clinic has three doctors and six nurses. A doctor is present during all
opening hours. At night and at weekends the clinic is staffed by a duty nurse,
who can call a doctor in if needed.
